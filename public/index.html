About Us

Aethra is a modern platform that connects customers with skilled metalworkers. We combine traditional craftsmanship with innovative technology to make custom metal designs easier to plan, visualize, and order online.

Our goal is to help independent metalworking businesses grow through digital innovation while supporting sustainable and modern design practices.

📍 Service Location

Aethra serves customers across selected service areas. Project availability, delivery arrangements, and installation options depend on the project requirements.

Available service areas may include:

  • North District
  • Central District
  • South District
  • West District
  • Nearby areas upon request

For projects outside our standard service areas, consultation and transportation arrangements can be discussed during booking.
